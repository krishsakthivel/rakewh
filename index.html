Module 02
Supply and Equilibrium

The supply curve shows the relationship between price and how much producers are willing to sell. As prices go up, producers want to sell more. The curve slopes upward.

Market equilibrium is the price where the amount supplied equals the amount people want to buy. This is the only stable point. If the price sits above it, there is surplus and it gets pushed back down. Below it, there is shortage and price climbs back up.

Supply shifts when something besides price changes, like better technology, lower input costs, or a change in government policy. A shift to the right means more is available at every price, which pushes the equilibrium price down and quantity sold up.
